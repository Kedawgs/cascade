FAQ

What is Cascade?

Cascade stakes TAO on Bittensor for you. You deposit USDC from a normal wallet on Base. Cascade swaps it to TAO, moves it to Bittensor and stakes it. Your balance shows your TAO and the rewards it earns.

Do I need a Bittensor wallet?

No. Everything happens from the wallet you already use on Base, such as MetaMask, Coinbase Wallet or Rabby.

Where is my TAO staked?

On Bittensor's root network with a vetted validator. Your balance comes with a live on-chain check you can open at any time.

What are the fees?

Every fee is shown before you confirm. A deposit carries the swap and bridge cost. Unstaking carries a small request fee that covers the trip back to Base.

How do I unstake?

Open the Unstake tab, enter an amount of TAO, and your USDC arrives in the same wallet on Base, usually within a day.

Is there a lock-up?

No. Unstaking takes a little time because the TAO has to be unstaked on Bittensor and sent back across to Base, but you can start it whenever you like.
