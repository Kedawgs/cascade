## Checklist item 1 — `getStake(hotkey, coldkey, 0)` units; does `removeStake(hotkey, rao, 0)` on root credit exactly `rao * 1e9` wei with no fee or slippage?

### Takeaway
Confirmed. `getStake` returns the raw `u64` position (alpha units; on root alpha is TAO rao 1:1). A root unstake takes the stable-mechanism branch (`mechanism_id == 0`): `amount_paid_out = alpha`, `fee_paid = 0`, and the TAO is credited to the caller's coldkey inside the same dispatch, so the contract's EVM balance rises by exactly `rao * 1e9` wei before the precompile call returns. Two caveats: requesting more than the position silently clips to the whole position (no revert), and share-pool rounding can make a *fresh* position read back 1 rao below what was sent.

### Cited Findings
- `get_stake` in the staking precompile returns `Ok(u64::from(stake).into())` where `stake = get_stake_for_hotkey_and_coldkey_on_subnet(&hotkey, &coldkey, netuid)`; no unit conversion. — [precompiles/src/staking.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/staking.rs)
- `swap_alpha_for_tao`: "Step 3.b.1: Stable mechanism, just return the value 1:1 … `amount_paid_out: alpha.to_u64().into(), fee_paid: AlphaBalance::ZERO, fee_to_block_author: AlphaBalance::ZERO`" when `SubnetMechanism::<T>::get(netuid) != 1`. — [pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)
- `unstake_from_subnet_with_flush_work` then calls `Self::transfer_tao_from_subnet(netuid, beneficiary, swap_result.amount_paid_out.into())?` and emits `StakeRemoved(... fee_paid ...)`. The beneficiary for `do_remove_stake` is the signing coldkey (`&coldkey, &coldkey`). — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs); [remove_stake.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/remove_stake.rs)
- Docs: root staking "converts 1:1 with no swap fee and no price impact." — [Bittensor docs: Money](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/concepts/money)
- EVM balance = substrate balance × 1e9: `into_evm_balance` does `value.checked_mul(U256::from(EVM_TO_SUBSTRATE_DECIMALS))` with `EVM_TO_SUBSTRATE_DECIMALS = 1_000_000_000`. — [runtime/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/runtime/src/lib.rs)
- `do_remove_stake`: "Cap the alpha_unstaked at available Alpha … `let alpha_unstaked = alpha_unstaked.min(alpha_available);`" — an over-request is clipped, not rejected. — [remove_stake.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/remove_stake.rs)
- Precompile `removeStake` passes `amount_alpha.unique_saturated_into()` as `u64` and dispatches `pallet_subtensor::Call::remove_stake` through `try_dispatch_stake_exit` with the caller's mirror as origin. — [staking.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/staking.rs)
- Field measurement by ForeverMoney on mainnet 964: "removeStake credits atomically in-tx; getStake is readable; addStake, moveStake and transferStakeFrom move the exact RAO asked for. transferStake is NOT exact. Creating a position that did not exist loses ONE RAO to share-pool rounding: 20,000,000 sent, 19,999,999 credited." Their `_removeStake` measures `address(this).balance` before/after the precompile call. — [ForeverMoney-Ai/bridge-contracts src/AlphaVault.sol](https://github.com/ForeverMoney-Ai/bridge-contracts/blob/main/src/AlphaVault.sol)
- Docs on truncation: "a position is stored as pool shares and reads back truncated, so the alpha the event reports can be one rao above what the position returns." — [Bittensor docs: Staking](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/staking)
- Live probe: `getStake(0x58aef7…, mirror(AlphaVault), 0)` returned `11,064,784,358,187` (rao, i.e. 11,064.78 TAO); `eth_estimateGas` for `removeStake(hk, 2_000_000, 0)` and for `removeStake(hk, 1_000_000_000, 0)` from the AlphaVault address both returned 144,811 gas. — [live probe, archive.chain.opentensor.ai, 2026-10-21](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai)

### Inferences
- The v1/v2 test double `removeStake(hotkey, rao, 0)` → `+rao*1e9 wei`, zero fee, is faithful for root. The vault should still measure the balance delta (ForeverMoney's pattern) rather than assume it, so a future mechanism change is detected rather than mis-accounted.
- Because an over-request clips to the full position, the v2 `processPayouts` formula `min(max(ceil(shortfall/1e9), MIN_STAKE_RAO), rootStakeRao())` is safe, but a bug that passes a too-large amount would drain the whole root position rather than revert.

### Gaps
- I did not measure `addStake` gas on mainnet: the dispatch fails `NotEnoughBalanceToStake` from any address without TAO, so `eth_estimateGas` cannot be run from a random address.

## Checklist item 2 — Are EVM balances and value transfers sub-rao precise, or truncated to rao?

### Takeaway
Everything is truncated to rao at the substrate boundary. EVM balances are always `rao * 1e9` wei. Value transfers (top-level `value`, `CALL` value, `msg.value` into a contract) are converted with floor division `value / 1e9`; a sub-rao remainder simply does not move, while `msg.value` inside the EVM still shows the full wei amount. Precompile credits arrive at rao precision. The 500-rao existential deposit is withheld once and is invisible on the EVM side.

### Cited Findings
- Frontier fork `transfer()`: "// Adjust decimals / `let value_sub = T::BalanceConverter::into_substrate_balance(EvmBalance::new(transfer.value)).ok_or(ExitError::OutOfFund)?;` `T::Currency::transfer(&source, &target, value_sub…, ExistenceRequirement::AllowDeath)`". — [vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/runner/stack.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/runner/stack.rs)
- `into_substrate_balance` is `value.checked_div(U256::from(EVM_TO_SUBSTRATE_DECIMALS))` (integer division, remainder dropped); `into_evm_balance` multiplies by 1e9. — [runtime/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/runtime/src/lib.rs)
- Fees are converted the same way (`into_substrate_balance(fee).ok_or(Error::<T>::FeeOverflow)`). — [vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/lib.rs)
- Docs: "Native TAO has 9 decimals … The EVM side uses 18 decimals … the existential deposit (500 rao) stays locked in it and is invisible from the EVM side … It's withheld once, not per transfer." — [Bittensor docs: EVM](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/evm)
- ForeverMoney enforces rao alignment on deposits: `if (msg.value % RAO != 0) revert NotWholeRao();` with `RAO = 1e9`, and the same on withdrawals (`wad % RAO`). — [AlphaVault.sol](https://github.com/ForeverMoney-Ai/bridge-contracts/blob/main/src/AlphaVault.sol)
- Live probe: `eth_estimateGas` for a plain transfer of `value: 0x1` (1 wei) returned 21,000 (the transaction is accepted; 0 rao moves). — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai)
- Precompile amount parameters are in rao; `getUnclaimedRootTaoByHotkey` is the exception and returns 18-decimal units via `tao_to_evm`. — [staking.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/staking.rs); [Bittensor docs: EVM](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/evm)

### Inferences
- Divergence from plain EVM: `msg.value` can overstate the credited amount by up to `1e9 - 1` wei. The v2 `onBridgeMessage` check `msg.value != amount → ValueMismatch` is fine only if the bridge adapter always forwards rao-aligned amounts; the vault should additionally require `amount % 1e9 == 0` (or round down and treat the remainder as dust) and compute `totalAssets()` from `address(this).balance`, which is rao-exact.
- A `redeemDirect` that sends `out` wei with a non-zero sub-rao part will under-pay by that remainder; rounding `out` down to a rao multiple in the vault makes the accounting match what actually moved.

### Gaps
- Existential-deposit reaping for contract mirrors: `ExistenceRequirement::AllowDeath` in `transfer()` means a contract whose free balance would fall below 500 rao could be reaped; I did not find a test or doc describing the EVM-side effect on a contract's storage/nonce (Frontier uses `NoncePreservingAccountProvider`, which suggests nonce survives). Treat "keep ≥ 500 rao in the vault at all times" as a precaution.

## Checklist item 3 — Do precompile failures surface as ordinary EVM reverts (catchable with `try/catch`)? Is `claimRootWithHotkey` with nothing to claim a no-op or a revert?

### Takeaway
Two different failure classes. (a) Input-validation failures inside the precompile (bad selector, oversize arrays, duplicates) are real `REVERT`s with `Error(string)` data and refund unused gas. (b) Runtime dispatch failures (`AmountTooLow`, `RootClaimTooHeavy`, `NotEnoughBalanceToStake`, …) are surfaced as `PrecompileFailure::Error { ExitError::Other("dispatch execution failed: <name>") }` — the sub-call fails with **no return data and all gas forwarded to it is consumed** (SputnikVM `swallow_discard` returns nothing to the parent). Solidity `try/catch` does take the catch branch, but after a 63/64 forward the caller has ~1/64 of its gas left. `claimRootWithHotkey` with nothing owed returns `Ok(())` (a no-op), but even the no-op must be given a gas limit of ~53.2M because the dispatch's declared weight is checked against the gas limit before execution.

### Cited Findings
- Dispatch failure path: "`Err(e) => { … Err(PrecompileFailure::Error { exit_status: ExitError::Other(format!("dispatch execution failed: {err_str}").into()) }) }`" and, before dispatch, "`if info.total_weight().ref_time() > valid_weight { return Err(PrecompileFailure::Error { exit_status: ExitError::OutOfGas }); }`" where `valid_weight = gas_to_weight(gas_limit)`. — [precompiles/src/extensions.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/extensions.rs)
- SputnikVM 0.43.4 (pinned in `Cargo.lock`): on `PrecompileFailure::Error` the executor calls `exit_substate(StackExitKind::Failed)` → `exit_discard` → `swallow_discard(&mut self, _other) { Ok(()) }` (no `record_stipend`), whereas `swallow_revert` does `self.gasometer.record_stipend(other.gasometer.gas())?`. — [evm 0.43.4 executor.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/evm/0.43.4/source/src/executor/stack/executor.rs)
- Input-validation reverts come from Frontier's precompile-utils (`RevertReason::UnknownSelector => "Unknown selector"`) and the precompile's own `revert("duplicate unclaimed root hotkey")`, `"hotkeys: Value is too large for length"`. — [vendor/frontier/precompiles/src/solidity/revert.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/vendor/frontier/precompiles/src/solidity/revert.rs); [staking.rs tests](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/staking.rs)
- Live probe shapes: `eth_call removeStake(0x0, 1, 0)` from a random address → `evm error: Other("dispatch execution failed: AmountTooLow")`, `data: 0x`; a wrong selector → `revert Unknown selector` with `data: 0x08c379a0…` (ABI `Error(string)`). — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai)
- Frame check: "`if !Self::accepts_foreign_frame(code_address) && code_address != handle.context().address { return Some(Err(PrecompileFailure::Error { exit_status: ExitError::Other("Cannot be called with DELEGATECALL or CALLCODE".into()) })); }`". — [precompiles/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/lib.rs)
- Precompile addresses have no bytecode (`eth_getCode(0x…0805) = 0x`, live probe) but Frontier reports code size 1 for them: "If the address is a precompile, we return size 1 and empty hash. Then the contract call bypass the code size check for precompile calls." So Solidity high-level calls to void functions do not trip the extcodesize check. — [vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/lib.rs)
- `claimRootWithHotkey` nothing-to-claim: "`let owed_shares = Self::get_basket_owed_shares(hotkey, coldkey); if owed_shares == 0 { return Ok(()); // no-op }`"; also `if shares_total == 0 { return Ok(()); }` and a below-threshold claim "`return Ok(()); // no-op`". — [pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs)
- Live probe: `eth_call claimRootWithHotkey(0x00…)` from a random address returned `0x` (success); `eth_estimateGas` for it returned **53,211,036** gas (random EOA) and 53,248,997 from the AlphaVault address on a zero-stake hotkey; `claimRoot([])` estimated **64,922,192**. Block gas limit is 75,000,000. — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai); [runtime/src/lib.rs `BLOCK_GAS_LIMIT`](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/runtime/src/lib.rs)
- Why so large: `claim_root_with_hotkey` declares `root_claim_hotkey_declared_weight()` = the 129-unit envelope (`MAX_ROOT_CLAIM_HOTKEY_WORK = 129`) × benchmarked `claim_root(h)` (437,318,379 ps per unit + 22 reads per unit) + `claim_root_scan(h)` + the flush allowance; the EVM path checks that declared weight against the gas limit up front and refunds the unused part afterwards (`charge_and_refund_after_dispatch`). — [claim_root.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs); [weights.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/weights.rs); [extensions.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/extensions.rs)
- Live probe on a real position: from the AlphaVault address, `claimRootWithHotkey(0x58aef7…)` (its 11,064-TAO root hotkey) and `claimRoot([])` both failed with `dispatch execution failed: RootClaimTooHeavy`, even with a 74M gas cap. The admission check counts the hotkey plus every raw `Alpha` and `AlphaV2` row under `(hotkey, beta-escrow)` and refuses if `work > 129` (single-hotkey) or `> 256` (coldkey-wide): "Count raw Alpha/AlphaV2 rows so legacy duplicates and malformed zero rows are charged conservatively". Error doc: "A root claim would process more root hotkeys and basket rows than the fixed admission envelope." — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai); [claim_root.rs `root_claim_fits_budget`](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs); [errors.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/errors.rs)
- Disabled precompiles "return a precompile-disabled error regardless of function lifecycle"; status is readable at the registry `0x…0813` via `getPrecompileStatus(address, bytes4)` (`isDisabled`). Live probe: `getPrecompileStatus(0x805, 0)` → `isDisabled = false`, 22,403 gas. — [Bittensor docs: Precompile design and lifecycle](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/evm/precompile-design); [precompiles/src/registry.rs `INDEX = 2067`](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/registry.rs)

### Inferences
- The v2 pattern `try STAKING.claimRootWithHotkey(hotkey) {} catch {}` inside `onBridgeMessage`/`processPayouts` does not work as designed on this chain: (i) the enclosing transaction must carry a ≥ ~53.2M gas limit (79% of a block) or the precompile returns `OutOfGas` before doing anything; (ii) a bridge adapter's delivery gas limit will not be that high; (iii) if the claim fails for a real reason (`RootClaimTooHeavy`, `BetaBasketSeedInProgress`), the forwarded gas is burned and the batch processing after it likely runs out of gas. Recommendation: make claiming a separate keeper transaction (`claimRewards()` public, as v2 already has), price batches from `rootStakeRao()` plus balance, and never put a claim in the bridge-delivery path. If a claim must be attempted inline, call it with an explicit `{gas: N}` sized from `eth_estimateGas` and check `gasleft()` first.
- Keepers can read the exact `DispatchError` name off-chain from `eth_call` (`"dispatch execution failed: X"`); on-chain code cannot, so a failed precompile call is indistinguishable from any other failed call. Emit the attempted operation and amount so the off-chain side can replay it with `eth_call`.
- `RootClaimTooHeavy` on a live 11k-TAO vault position today (inference about cause: the validator's fund carries legacy `Alpha` rows alongside `AlphaV2` rows, which double-count against the 129 budget; v473's "Deprecate alpha v1 - stage 1" suggests cleanup is in progress) means a vault can be unable to realise rewards for an unknown period through no action of its own. Pricing shares without unclaimed rewards (v2's conservative `totalAssets`) is the right default; the hotkey choice should include "basket row count stays well under 128".

### Gaps
- I could not test how Solidity's `try/catch` observes an `ExitError` sub-call on this exact runtime (no deployable test contract in this session); the gas-consumption conclusion comes from the SputnikVM source, not an on-chain measurement.
- No doc states when the AlphaVault hotkey's basket will fit the budget again, or whether `RootClaimTooHeavy` is widespread across validators.

## Checklist item 4 — Current `DefaultMinStake`, the exact `AmountTooLow` rule for partial vs whole-position unstakes, and whether sub-minimum leftover root positions are swept to the coldkey balance

### Takeaway
Live `DefaultMinStake = 2,000,000 rao` (0.002 TAO). Add: `amount ≥ min_stake + sim_swap fee` (fee 0 on root). Remove: amount must be non-zero; if the position does not go to zero, the TAO paid out must be ≥ 0.002 TAO; a full exit is exempt from the floor; an over-request clips to full exit. After every `remove_stake`, if the coldkey does not own the hotkey and the remainder is below the nominator minimum — live `getNominatorMinRequiredStake = 20,000,000 rao` (0.02 TAO), ten times the min stake — the whole remainder is force-unstaked (no fee, no root-hold check) to the coldkey's free balance. Moves use different floors: cross-subnet `> DefaultMinStake`, same-subnet `≥ DefaultMinTransfer = 100,000 rao`.

### Cited Findings
- Live probe: `getDefaultMinStake()` → `2,000,000`; `getNominatorMinRequiredStake()` → `20,000,000`. — [live probe](https://lite.chain.opentensor.ai)
- Runtime constants: `SubtensorInitialMinStake = 2_000_000 // 0.002 tao`, `SubtensorInitialMinTransfer = 100_000 // 0.0001 tao`. — [runtime/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/runtime/src/lib.rs)
- `validate_add_stake`: `min_amount = min_stake + fee` (fee from `sim_swap` or `approx_fee_amount`); "`ensure!(stake_to_be_added >= min_amount, Error::<T>::AmountTooLow)`" and `ensure!(swap_result.amount_paid_in >= min_stake, AmountTooLow)`. — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)
- `validate_remove_stake`: "`ensure!(!alpha_unstaked.is_zero(), Error::<T>::AmountTooLow)`"; then "Bypass this check if the user unstakes full amount": `if !remaining_alpha_stake.is_zero() { ensure!(res.amount_paid_out >= DefaultMinStake::<T>::get(), AmountTooLow) }`. — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)
- Live probe: `removeStake(hk, 1_000_000, 0)` (0.001 TAO) from the AlphaVault → `AmountTooLow`; `removeStake(hk, 2_000_000, 0)` → 144,811 gas (accepted). — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai)
- Sweep: `clear_small_nomination_if_required` runs after `do_remove_stake` ("5. If the stake is below the minimum, we clear the nomination from storage"): if `!coldkey_owns_hotkey`, `min_alpha_stake = nominator_min_required_stake / current_alpha_price`, and `if alpha_stake > 0 && alpha_stake < min_alpha_stake` it calls `unstake_from_subnet(hotkey, coldkey, coldkey, netuid, alpha_stake, min_price, drop_fees=false, enforce_root_hold=false)` — "Do not apply any fees", errors ignored. — [pallets/subtensor/src/staking/helpers.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/helpers.rs); [remove_stake.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/remove_stake.rs)
- `get_nominator_min_required_stake() = DefaultMinStake * NominatorMinRequiredStake(per-million factor) / 1_000_000`. — [pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/utils/misc.rs)
- Moves/transfers: cross-subnet "`ensure!(tao_equivalent > min_amount, AmountTooLow)`" with `min_amount = DefaultMinTransfer` for transfers and `DefaultMinStake` for moves; same-subnet `transfer_stake_within_subnet` requires "`tao_equivalent >= DefaultMinTransfer`". `AlphaBalance::MAX` "means 'the live origin position', on every call that debits a position: `move_stake*`, `transfer_stake*` and `swap_stake*`." — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs); [move_stake.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/move_stake.rs)
- Docs wording ("unstakes are held to the same floor, both on the amount withdrawn and on any dust position left behind") describes the outcome, not a revert: the code sweeps the dust rather than rejecting the unstake. — [Bittensor docs: Money](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/concepts/money)

### Inferences
- For the vault's hotkey (which it will not own), a partial root unstake that leaves `0 < remainder < 0.02 TAO` succeeds and is immediately followed by a sweep of the remainder into the contract's native balance. `rootStakeRao()` then reads 0 while the balance is higher than expected; v2's `totalAssets = stake*1e9 + balance − pendingPayouts` handles this, but any invariant of the form "stake only changes by what we asked" will break. The v2 floor rule should use `max(minStakeRao, nominatorMinRao)` = 0.02 TAO as the "leave at least this much or exit fully" threshold, read live via `getNominatorMinRequiredStake()`.
- Because `removeStake` clips rather than reverts, "unstake everything" can be expressed as `removeStake(hotkey, type(uint64).max, 0)` or `removeStakeFull(hotkey, 0)` (measured 147,852 gas).

### Gaps
- `NominatorMinRequiredStake` is governance-set; no doc lists its history or planned changes.

## Checklist item 5 — Semantics of `setRejectStakingHotkeyGrowth(true)`; can anyone send native TAO to the contract's mirror bypassing `receive()`?

### Takeaway
The flag only blocks *third-party* operations that would append a **new** hotkey to the contract's `StakingHotkeys` list: `transfer_stake`/`transferStakeFrom` into the contract and hotkey swaps that would rename onto a new entry. Transfers onto a hotkey the contract already stakes to (or owns) still succeed, and nothing stops native TAO arriving. Yes — anyone can `Balances.transfer_keep_alive` TAO to the contract's ss58 mirror; that is a substrate balance mutation that runs no EVM code, so `receive()` never executes. Precompile unstake credits arrive the same way.

### Cited Findings
- Flag bit and setter: `ACCOUNT_FLAGS_REJECT_STAKING_HOTKEY_GROWTH: u128 = 1 << 1`; `set_reject_staking_hotkey_growth(enabled)` mutates `AccountFlags` for the signer and emits `RejectStakingHotkeyGrowthUpdated`. Precompile `setRejectStakingHotkeyGrowth(bool)` dispatches it with `RawOrigin::Signed(caller mirror)`; `getRejectStakingHotkeyGrowth(bytes32)` reads it. — [pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs); [dispatches.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/macros/dispatches.rs); [staking.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/staking.rs)
- Enforcement sites: `ensure_third_party_staking_hotkeys_can_grow`: "`if hotkeys.contains(hotkey) { return Ok(()); } ensure!(!account_rejects_staking_hotkey_growth(coldkey) || coldkey_owns_hotkey(coldkey, hotkey), AccountRejectsStakingHotkeyGrowth); ensure!(hotkeys.len() < MAX_THIRD_PARTY_STAKING_HOTKEYS, TooManyStakingHotkeys)`", called from `validate_stake_transition` only when `origin_coldkey != destination_coldkey`; and `hotkey_swap_stake_can_follow` for hotkey swaps by other coldkeys. The coldkey's own staking uses `ensure_staking_hotkeys_can_grow` (cap `MAX_STAKING_HOTKEYS = 256`), which ignores the flag. — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)

### Inferences
- With the flag set, a stranger can still `transfer_stake` root stake **onto the vault's current hotkey** (it is on the list), which raises `rootStakeRao()` and so the share price — a donation, same class as a native transfer. The flag's real value is bounding the `StakingHotkeys` list, which every unstake-side dispatch walks (gas) and which the coldkey-wide claim refuses above budget. Keep it on.
- The v2 `receive()` revert blocks only EVM-level sends (wallet transfers, other contracts); it cannot block substrate-level donations or precompile credits. v2's "donations only raise the price" stance is therefore necessary, not optional. Do not make `_removeStake` depend on `receive()` being callable: today it is not invoked, and ForeverMoney's windowed `receive()` is the defensive version if a future runtime routes credits through EVM value transfers.

### Gaps
- Whether limit-order fills (`order_swap.rs` touches `to_coldkey`) can deliver stake to a third-party coldkey and whether they consult the flag was not traced.

## Checklist item 6 — `moveStake(old, new, 0, 0, rao)` root-to-root; precompile from a constructor; mirror computation; learning the contract's own coldkey on-chain

### Takeaway
Root-to-root `moveStake` is permitted, fee-free and slippage-free (same-subnet path), requires `≥ 0.0001 TAO`, is blocked by the root hold if `RootStakeUnlockInterval > 0`, flushes both hotkeys' queued dividends first, does **not** claim accrued basket yield, and measured 121,699 gas from a live vault. The mirror is exactly `blake2_256("evm:" ++ h160)` (verified live against the `0x…080c` precompile), computable off-chain with `btcli evm mirror` and on-chain with `addressMapping(address(this))` (~22k gas). Calling precompiles from a constructor should work (the caller is `address(this)` during CREATE, as for any CALL), but this is inference — test on 945 first.

### Cited Findings
- `validate_stake_transition`: `SameNetuid` only if same coldkey **and** same hotkey; same-subnet moves skip the min-stake/slippage block and go to `transfer_stake_within_subnet` ("Does not incur any swapping nor fees"), which flushes basket deposits for both hotkeys on root, requires `tao_equivalent >= DefaultMinTransfer`, and `touch_root_stake_age(destination_coldkey, destination_hotkey)`; root hold: "any user transition that removes stake from root must wait out the interval … `if origin_netuid.is_root() { ensure_root_stake_unlocked(origin_coldkey, origin_hotkey)?; }`". — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)
- `do_move_stake` caps `AlphaBalance::MAX` to the live position and dispatches `transition_stake_internal`; the fee logic (`drop_fee_origin = origin_netuid == ROOT`) only applies on the cross-subnet branch. — [move_stake.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/move_stake.rs)
- Docs: "Moving a position between hotkeys on the same subnet (move-stake) is *not* a swap — no fee, no price impact." Root: "Root unstake returns principal, and basket yield stays owed"; `btcli root move` "claims accrued yield on the source before moving the position." — [Bittensor docs: Staking and pools](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/concepts/staking-pools); [Root Reborn](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/root-reborn); [Basket trading for stakers](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/basket-trading-for-stakers)

- CLI: `btcli evm mirror 0xCONTRACT… --json | jq -r .ss58_mirror`. — [Bittensor docs: Stake from the EVM](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/evm/stake-from-evm)
- ForeverMoney stores the coldkey as an immutable and notes "`targetColdkey` is supplied rather than derived because the EVM has no blake2b precompile … which is exactly the mismatch that produced a duplicate vault storing another vault's coldkey during the v3 deploy." — [AlphaVault.sol](https://github.com/ForeverMoney-Ai/bridge-contracts/blob/main/src/AlphaVault.sol)

### Inferences
- The v2 constructor should derive `coldkey` by calling `IAddressMapping(0x…080c).addressMapping(address(this))` rather than taking it as a parameter — this removes the exact deploy-time mismatch ForeverMoney hit, and the blake2 "no on-chain hash" objection does not apply because the precompile does it. Keep the constructor parameter only as a cross-check (`require(param == derived)`).
- `setHotkey` must claim before moving (v2 already does): a `moveStake` leaves the old hotkey's basket entitlement owed, and the claim on the old hotkey later lands as root stake **on the old hotkey**, not the new one, so the vault must keep tracking the old hotkey until that claim succeeds (and must give that claim the 53M-gas envelope).
- Constructor calls to `setRejectStakingHotkeyGrowth(true)` cost ~26k gas and need no TAO balance; `addStake` in a constructor would need the contract pre-funded (CREATE with value is fine since value is rao-floored).

### Gaps
- No primary source explicitly covers precompile calls during contract construction on this chain; verify on testnet 945.

## Checklist item 7 — Root unstake "too early" windows; `claim_root` cadence/limits

### Takeaway
`RootStakeUnlockInterval` is **0 on mainnet today** (storage unset → default 0 → hold disabled). If governance sets it > 0, every exit off root for a `(coldkey, hotkey)` pair — `remove_stake`, move/swap/transfer off root — fails with `RootStakeLocked` until `interval` blocks have passed since the pair's last root add, remove, claim payout, limit-order fill or incoming same-subnet transfer. There is no claim rate limit; claims are manual, have no deadline, skip below a 0.0005-TAO threshold, and can be refused as `RootClaimTooHeavy`.

### Cited Findings
- Storage doc: "`0` disables the hold (default), preserving legacy behaviour. When set >= one tempo it neutralises epoch-boundary 'just-in-time' dividend sniping". `LastColdkeyHotkeyStakeBlock` is "the last block at which **root** stake was added/removed/claimed for that pair … Non-root stake ops must not write this map." — [pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs)
- Live probe: `state_getStorage(twox128("SubtensorModule") ++ twox128("RootStakeUnlockInterval"))` → `null` (unset → `ValueQuery` default 0) at block ~9,329,5xx; the same key scheme returned `BasketTradingEnabled = 0x01` and `TotalIssuance ≈ 11,640,736 TAO`, so the scheme is right. — [live probe](https://lite.chain.opentensor.ai)
- `ensure_root_stake_unlocked`: "No-op when the interval is `0`"; otherwise `ensure!(now - last >= interval, RootStakeLocked)`. `touch_root_stake_age` call sites: unstake (`stake_utils.rs:1166`), stake-in (`:1270`), same-subnet transfer destination (`:1395`), claim payout (`claim_root.rs:891`), limit-order fill (`order_swap.rs:159`), collateral capture (`collateral.rs:386`). — [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs); [claim_root.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs)
- Docs: `RootStakeLocked` "Applies to `remove_stake` and to move/swap/transfer off root"; remedy "wait some blocks and retry" or sudo sets the interval to 0. — [Bittensor docs: RootStakeLocked](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/errors/chain/RootStakeLocked)
- Dust cleanup bypasses the hold: `clear_small_nomination_if_required` passes `enforce_root_hold = false`. — [helpers.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/helpers.rs)
- Root-stake pauses: `BetaBasketSeedInProgress` ("root stake add/remove/transfer/swap are paused until it finishes" during a migration) via `ensure_beta_basket_seed_idle()` in `validate_add_stake`/`validate_remove_stake`/`validate_stake_transition`. — [Bittensor docs: too_early](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/errors/too-early); [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)
- Claims: "There is no deadline and nothing expires"; "Auto-claim: Off"; threshold "default is 500,000 rao (τ0.0005), capped at τ0.01"; fee "around τ0.057 on a full 128-holding basket" (native path). Rate limits are documented only for root registrations. — [Bittensor docs: Root Reborn](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/root-reborn)
- No `sudo_set_root_stake_unlock_interval` exists in `pallets/admin-utils` on `main` (grep returned nothing), so changing it needs a runtime upgrade or a raw sudo storage write. — [pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)

### Inferences
- Today a contract can stake and unstake root in the same block. The v2 note "a precompile revert, e.g. root 'too early', bubbles up — retry later" is correct behaviour if the interval is ever turned on, but note that `stakeIdle()` after every batch would then re-arm the hold on the vault's hotkey each time, so with a non-zero interval the vault could be perpetually locked by its own deposits. Mitigation if that happens: stake idle TAO on a cadence (not per batch) or keep a cash buffer sized to `interval` worth of redemptions.
- Because claims touch the age only when they pay out, a no-op claim does not re-arm the hold.

### Gaps
- No announcement of an intended non-zero `RootStakeUnlockInterval` was found; the storage comment implies it may be set to "≥ one tempo" (360 blocks) at some point.

## Checklist item 8 — Root reward mechanics in Oct 2026: claimable continuously? typical root APR? is basket trading live? does it change how a contract's root stake earns?

### Takeaway
Rewards accrue per epoch into the validator's basket fund (as subnet alpha); the staker's entitlement is a pro-rata share of the fund and is realised only on claim, and claim proceeds are **re-staked to root on the same hotkey under the claimant's coldkey** (not paid to balance). `BasketTradingEnabled` is **true on mainnet** (live storage; v468 "enable basket trading"), contradicting the docs page that still says testnet-only. v481 (Oct 19) pays root "its live baseline share off the top every block". No primary source gives a current root APR; one live datapoint: the ForeverMoney vault's 11,064.78 TAO root position had 55.67 TAO unclaimed on 2026-10-21.

### Cited Findings
- Accrual and claim: "Each epoch, your dividends are credited to your validator's fund as subnet alpha, and stakers accrue a share pro-rata to their root stake … realized only when a staker claims"; `claim_root(subnets)`: "`subnets` is ignored and the call claims fund-level across every validator"; "Claiming converts entitlement into root stake on that validator." — [Bittensor docs: Root Reborn](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/root-reborn)
- Live probe: `BasketTradingEnabled` storage = `0x01` (true). Storage doc: "Master switch for `swap_basket` … Defaults to OFF. Flipped on via `AdminUtils::sudo_set_basket_trading_enabled`. Gates only the trade path: deposits, claims, dividend accrual, and all read paths are unaffected." — [live probe](https://lite.chain.opentensor.ai); [pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/lib.rs); [Release v468](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/releases/tag/v468)
- Docs still say "Trading is live on **testnet** and not on mainnet yet" (stale relative to the live flag). Guardrails: each leg within 2% of moving and spot price, turnover budget 10% of NAV per day, no holding above 1/16 of NAV or 10% of a pool's alpha, governance can freeze a fund. "A trade changes what the fund holds, not what you own." — [Bittensor docs: Basket trading for stakers](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/basket-trading-for-stakers)
- v481: "etao: pay root its live baseline share off the top every block"; v478: "Size the stake-exit walk to the caller's list in the EVM and contract paths"; v469: "failed-call fee refunds"; v468: "enable basket trading + skip dust rows on root claims". — [subtensor releases](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/releases)
- Claim mechanics in code: owed shares → dust rows consolidated → rows valued at realizable NAV → payout estimate compared with `RootClaimableThreshold(ROOT)` → "TAO credited to the staker's root stake"; basket flush of queued dividends runs before every root stake-in/out so "a staker doesn't forfeit flushable dividends earned while they were staked". — [claim_root.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs); [stake_utils.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/stake_utils.rs)
- ForeverMoney's reading: "Claim proceeds ALWAYS land on root (netuid 0) -- the runtime sells the basket and stakes it there, and the destination is not a parameter"; "The documented automatic rotation was not observable on chain (400 blocks scanned, every RootClaimed came from a signed extrinsic)"; "the root claim has no slippage bound at all -- so an attacker could pick a manipulated moment", which is why they gate claims to an operator. — [AlphaVault.sol](https://github.com/ForeverMoney-Ai/bridge-contracts/blob/main/src/AlphaVault.sol)
- Live probe: `getUnclaimedRootTaoByHotkey(mirror(AlphaVault), 0x58aef7…)` = `55,674,344,413,000,000,000` wei (55.67 TAO) against 11,064.78 TAO staked; the view estimates 1,699,302 gas. — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai)
- Precompile exposure: only `claimRoot(uint16[])`, `claimRootWithHotkey(bytes32)`, `getUnclaimedRootTaoByHotkey`, `getUnclaimedRootTaoBySubnet`; no `stake_into_basket`/`swap_basket` from EVM. — [stakingV2.sol](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/solidity/stakingV2.sol)

### Inferences
- "Claimable continuously": entitlement grows each epoch (~72 min) and can be claimed at any time, subject to the threshold and the admission budget; there is no epoch-aligned window to hit.
- For a contract, basket trading changes the *value* of what is owed (NAV moves with the validator's trades and subnet prices) but not the mechanism: principal stays 1:1 TAO on root; yield is a claim on a validator-curated alpha fund realised at claim-time pool prices with no slippage bound. Choosing the hotkey is therefore choosing a fund manager; v2 should expose `getUnclaimedRootTaoByHotkey` to keepers and claim on a schedule (and whenever the owed amount is large relative to expected slippage), never inside batch delivery.
- APR: 55.67 / 11,064.78 ≈ 0.50% unclaimed; without the accrual start date this is not an APR. Treat prior-note figures (4–7%) as unverified for the post-etao regime.

### Gaps
- No primary source for current root APR under v481's "baseline share off the top". Taostats' yield page is the candidate source; not retrieved.
- Whether a contract's claim is affected by the 128-row basket cap differently from EOAs: no (same dispatch), but the `RootClaimTooHeavy` incidence across validators is unknown.

### Inferences
- Cost model for the vault: at the 5 gwei floor, gas costs 5 rao; 1M gas = 0.005 TAO. A batch of 100 mints (~50k each) + redeems + `addStake` ≈ 5.5M gas ≈ 0.0275 TAO. If the batch also had to carry a `claimRootWithHotkey` envelope, the transaction's gas *limit* would need to be ~59M (actual charge lower), which is 79% of a block and far beyond any cross-chain executor's delivery gas cap — a second reason to keep claims out of delivery.
- The "0.8.24 / Cancun" doc rule is conservative: the runtime's EVM is Pectra, and a live contract runs on 0.8.26/paris. Using `evm_version = "cancun"` with any 0.8.2x compiler is the safe choice; avoid `prague`/EOF targets until the docs change.
- Block space is empty today (0–200k gas used of 75M), so gas price stays at the floor and inclusion is immediate; plan for the base fee to rise only if usage exceeds 50% of block weight.

### Gaps
- No published numeric rate limit for the public endpoints; the 429 observation is from one probe. For production, use a paid provider (dRPC/OnFinality) or run a node.

## Governance and upgrade risk for an immutable contract

### Takeaway
Everything that can break the vault is behind sudo (Rao Foundation multisig): precompile disable/enable (`sudo_toggle_evm_precompile`), basket trading, claim thresholds, min-stake factors, and runtime upgrades roughly weekly (ten runtime versions in the four weeks to Oct 19). There is no notice period in the lifecycle policy. Precedents in 2026 of upgrades changing behaviour under existing selectors or call paths: `claimRoot(subnets)` now ignores its argument (v441); delegatecall into precompiles rejected (v452); contract→proxy `transfer_stake` broken for a day (v453→454, ink! path); EVM gas sizing for stake exits changed (v478); fee halving (v467); failed-call refunds (v469); basket trading switched on (v468). No evidence that any 2026 upgrade broke a direct EVM precompile call from a plain contract.

### Cited Findings
- `sudo_toggle_evm_precompile(origin, …)`: `ensure_root(origin)?`; `sudo_set_basket_trading_enabled`: `ensure_root`. — [pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/admin-utils/src/lib.rs)
- Lifecycle: "Root, through `AdminUtils.sudo_toggle_evm_precompile`"; "This switch is reversible and applies to the precompile as a whole"; no fixed notice period ("after a documented migration window" only for raw storage access). — [Precompile design and lifecycle](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/evm/precompile-design)
- Release notes: v478 "Size the stake-exit walk to the caller's list in the EVM and contract paths"; v473 "Deprecate alpha v1 - stage 1", "Publish mainnet releases without a second approval"; v469 "failed-call fee refunds"; v468 "enable basket trading"; v461 "set_root_weights is removed"; v445 "completes the typed EVM surface"; v439 "EVM access to stake locks". — [subtensor releases](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/releases); [bittensor.com/releases](https://www.bittensor.com/releases)
- v452 frame check (PR #3130) and the 453/454 regression (PR #3140): see items 3 and prior-art findings. — [PR #3130](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3130); [PR #3140](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/3140)
- Root Reborn changed `claim_root`'s meaning under the same signature: "`claim_root(subnets)` keeps its old signature, but … `subnets` is ignored". — [Root Reborn](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/root-reborn)

### Inferences
- The vault should treat every precompile call as "may fail or change semantics after any Monday upgrade": read parameters live (`getDefaultMinStake`, `getNominatorMinRequiredStake`, `getPrecompileStatus`), keep a guardian pause and a permissionless `unstakeAll`/`removeStakeFull` exit, and run a testnet (945) regression of the exact precompile calls against each "proposed" pre-release before it hits mainnet (pre-releases are published days ahead on GitHub).
- The immutable-contract risk that cannot be mitigated in code: a disabled staking precompile would strand stake until re-enabled; the only recourse is governance. Document this in user-facing risk disclosures.

### Gaps
- Multisig signer identities and any formal change-notice commitment were not found (same gap as the prior notes).

## Divergences between the real chain and simple EVM assumptions (summary for the builder)

### Takeaway
Ten places where "it's just an EVM" is wrong for CascadeVault, each with the source above.

### Cited Findings
- Balances are rao-granular: `msg.value` may exceed what actually moved by up to `1e9 − 1` wei; contract balances are always multiples of 1e9 wei. — [runner/stack.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/runner/stack.rs)
- Precompile dispatch failures are `ExitError`, not `REVERT`: no revert data on-chain, and the gas forwarded to the call is burned; `try/catch` catches but leaves ~1/64 gas. — [extensions.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/extensions.rs); [evm 0.43.4 executor.rs](https://docs.rs/crate/evm/0.43.4/source/src/executor/stack/executor.rs)
- A precompile call's gas *limit* must cover the extrinsic's declared weight even when the actual work is small: ~53.2M gas for `claimRootWithHotkey`, ~64.9M for `claimRoot`, against a 75M block. — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai)
- `removeStake` with an amount above the position silently exits the whole position; amounts below 0.002 TAO (unless full exit) revert; a remainder below 0.02 TAO on a non-owned hotkey is force-swept to the contract's balance with no event the contract can observe in-tx. — [remove_stake.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/remove_stake.rs); [helpers.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/helpers.rs)
- Native TAO and unstake credits arrive by substrate balance mutation: `receive()` never runs for them and cannot block donations. — [AlphaVault.sol](https://github.com/ForeverMoney-Ai/bridge-contracts/blob/main/src/AlphaVault.sol); [fund-evm-key](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/tx/fund-evm-key)
- Root rewards are not paid to the contract: a claim re-stakes them as root stake on the same hotkey, so `rootStakeRao()` grows and the balance does not. — [Root Reborn](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/root-reborn)
- A claim can be refused outright (`RootClaimTooHeavy`) for reasons outside the contract's control (the validator's basket row count); the live ForeverMoney position is in that state today. — [live probe](https://archive.chain.opentensor.ai); [claim_root.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/pallets/subtensor/src/staking/claim_root.rs)
- Precompile addresses have no bytecode (`eth_getCode` = `0x`) yet `EXTCODESIZE` reports 1; delegatecall/callcode into them fails; a disabled precompile fails all calls. — [vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/vendor/frontier/frame/evm/src/lib.rs); [precompiles/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/precompiles/src/lib.rs)
- Priority fees are ignored (clamped to zero effect) and recycled; there is no fee market to outbid a sandwich, and the base fee floors at 5 gwei. — [PR #2307](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/pull/2307); [runtime/src/lib.rs](https://github.com/RaoFoundation/subtensor/blob/main/runtime/src/lib.rs)
- Semantics under a fixed selector can change by runtime upgrade with no notice period (e.g., `claimRoot` argument ignored since v441). — [Root Reborn](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/root-reborn); [Precompile design and lifecycle](https://www.bittensor.com/docs/guides/evm/precompile-design)

### Inferences
- Concrete v2 design changes implied: (1) remove `_claimRewards()` from `onBridgeMessage`/`processPayouts`; make claiming a keeper call with an explicit gas budget and an off-chain pre-check; (2) require `amount % 1e9 == 0` for bridged deposits and round `out` down to rao in payouts; (3) derive the coldkey via `0x…080c` in the constructor; (4) use `max(getDefaultMinStake(), getNominatorMinRequiredStake())` as the leave-behind floor and prefer `removeStakeFull` for exits; (5) treat `rootStakeRao()` decreases without a vault-initiated unstake as a sweep, not an attack, and never pause on them; (6) measure balance deltas around every precompile call; (7) keep ≥ 500 rao of free balance; (8) gate on `getPrecompileStatus(0x805).isDisabled` in keepers and surface it in the UI.

### Gaps
- All gas figures are `eth_estimateGas` results at spec 481 on 2026-10-21 and will move with every weight re-benchmark; re-measure before each release.
